The concept

A restaurant built around discovery.

ALTINTERIM brings together selected dishes inspired by the world's great food cultures, a sea-facing terrace and a modern service experience.

The first house is imagined as a flagship in Baku — the beginning of a brand designed to grow carefully, location by location.

~140planned seats
1main floor + terrace
5culinary worlds
2–3service robots in the concept
The experience

Sea views. Human hospitality. Smart service.

Technology supports the team — it does not replace the feeling of being welcomed.

01 · TERRACE

Sea-facing dining

A generous outdoor terrace for lunch, sunset and long dinners by the Caspian Sea.

02 · SERVICE

Human + technology

QR ordering, POS integration and a small number of service robots help the team move efficiently.

03 · MUSIC

Weekend nights

A compact terrace stage for Friday and Saturday live performances while keeping dinner comfortable.

The long-term vision

Baku is the beginning.

ALTINTERIM is designed to become a repeatable restaurant system: strong hospitality, disciplined operations, recognizable design and a clear brand identity.

01 · FLAGSHIP

Prove the concept

Open the first house and build a loyal local audience.

02 · SYSTEM

Build the playbook

Standardize kitchen, service, technology, training and finance.

03 · GROW

Open selectively

Expand only when the unit economics and management team are ready.

04 · GLOBAL

Take ALTINTERIM abroad

Evaluate cities such as Istanbul and Dubai when the brand is ready.
